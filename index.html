About Me


I’m Jemima — a personal trainer who believes fitness is about so much more than physical change. Over the last two years, training has transformed my life. It’s helped me build confidence, improve my mental wellbeing, and feel stronger in every part of my life.

I know exactly how intimidating the gym can feel. I’ve been the person who didn’t know where to start, who worried about doing things “wrong,” and who felt out of place. That’s why my mission is to make fitness feel welcoming, empowering, and enjoyable for you.

Whether your goal is to get stronger, lose weight, build muscle, or simply begin your journey, I’m here to guide you with patience, clarity, and support. Together, we’ll build habits that last, celebrate your progress, and help you feel confident in your body and your abilities.

Let’s make fitness a positive part of your life — one step at a time ❤️
